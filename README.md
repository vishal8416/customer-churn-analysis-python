# Customer Churn Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

Customer churn is one of the most critical challenges faced by businesses, as retaining existing customers is often more cost-effective than acquiring new ones. This project focuses on analyzing customer behavior to identify the key factors that contribute to customer attrition and provide actionable recommendations to improve customer retention.

Using Python and Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) techniques, this project examines customer demographics, contract types, tenure, service subscriptions, and revenue-related metrics to uncover churn patterns and business insights.

---

## 🎯 Objective

Analyze customer behavior and identify key factors driving customer churn to support data-driven retention strategies and improve customer loyalty.

## 🔍 Key Findings

### Customer Churn Rate
- Approximately **26–27%** of customers have churned.
- Around **73–74%** of customers remain active.

### Demographic Insights
- Senior citizens exhibit significantly higher churn rates.
- Gender has minimal impact on customer churn.

### Contract Analysis
- Month-to-month customers show the highest churn rate.
- Long-term contracts significantly improve customer retention.

### Service Subscription Analysis
Customers without:
- Online Security
- Tech Support
- Online Backup

are substantially more likely to churn.

### Customer Value Analysis
- Customers with shorter tenure are more likely to leave.
- Long-term customers generate greater value and show stronger retention.

---

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations

### 1. Promote Long-Term Contracts
Offer discounts and incentives for customers transitioning from month-to-month plans to annual contracts.

### 2. Bundle Retention Services
Encourage adoption of:
- Online Security
- Tech Support
- Online Backup

to increase customer stickiness.

### 3. Focus on Senior Citizens
Develop targeted support programs and personalized plans for senior customers.

### 4. Improve New Customer Onboarding
Implement proactive engagement strategies during the first year of customer lifecycle.
